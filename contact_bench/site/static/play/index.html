Rules in brief
  • Two guessers try to reach a secret word that the Defender knows. Words are from the ENABLE dictionary (lowercase a–z).
  • Guesser 1 gives a public clue (printable ASCII, at most the shown number of characters) and privately registers an intended word that starts with the prefix and is not used, or makes a direct guess of the secret.
  • Guesser 2 privately registers a word for the clue; at the same time the Defender publishes a blocklist (words and alphabetical ranges).
  • If both guessers registered the same unused word, it is not blocked and is not in the clue, the next letter of the secret is revealed.
  • If exactly one guesser's word is blocked, the other chooses to reveal or cancel theirs.
  • Saying the secret anywhere in public wins, including in a clue or on the Defender's blocklist. Every public word becomes used.
  • Cost is the winning turn, or the turn cap plus a reference estimate of the turns still needed. In each fixture you win if your attacking cost is lower than the model's.

Pausing keeps every recorded move; resume with contact-bench play --resume RUN_ID.
